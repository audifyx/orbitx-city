and reporting. The voxel avatars in `characters.json` are built from this
sheet. Where a real face could not be verified, the build follows the
person's PUBLIC AVATAR instead, and the entry says so — never invent a face.

> **These are real people. Their yes is needed before launch.** This sheet is
> build-only reference. Do not ship a likeness anyone has not agreed to.
