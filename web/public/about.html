OrbitX City

What is OrbitX City?

OrbitX City is a free realistic city life-sim that runs entirely in your browser. No download, no install — open orbitxcity.world and you are in the city.

Play free in your browser

Life in the city

You arrive as a guest or sign up free, then live in a tropical downtown realistic skyline. Everything happens in one persistent multiplayer world with real players and live voice chat.

JobsWork around the city and earn City Sol, the in-game currency.
HomesBuy a home and make it yours in one of 12 districts.
CarsDrive 33 vehicles, including flyers, across the city.
RacesRace other players, including the Plaza Sprint.
BoxingStep into the ring and box other players.
Mini-gamesArcade games and challenges all over the city.
Voice chatTalk with players in the world and at live events in the HQ Event Hall.
Phone OSAn in-game phone with apps, messages and the city map.

How to play

Open orbitxcity.world in any modern browser on phone or computer. Play instantly as a guest, or sign up free to keep your home, job and progress. There is nothing to download and the game is free.

Part of OrbitX

OrbitX City is built by OrbitX, a free Solana super-app with a token scanner, DEX, launchpad and AI trading agent. OrbitX is free forever — scanning tokens and using the platform costs nothing.

Not to be confused with

OrbitX City is not OrbCity (the ORB token LandFi metaverse) and not orbitx.ai (a space data company). OrbitX City is the free browser realistic city game at orbitxcity.world, made by OrbitX.
